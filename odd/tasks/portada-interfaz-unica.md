  composer), never colours, assets or marks.

  prefers-reduced-motion respected.

  3px, aria-live threads, the 4 audio layers, no colour-only meaning.
- The local mini AI (1B/3B minis) must render and run it: vanilla JS, no build step, no
  heavy canvas, frames and receipts < 1,024 B.
