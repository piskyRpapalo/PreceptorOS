### The law of the adaptation (binding)
- Alan did not rewrite an agent; he gave an existing one a new face. The same here, in our
  direction: the face EVOLVES (this portada), the brain ALREADY EXISTS (hexelion + the
  harness + la mente). Nothing of his stack enters this repo: no Tauri, no daemon, no Node
  dependencies, no WebSocket protocol, no rose, no neon pink, no imported palette.

  local mini or the loopback station, honest receipt per reply: model · duration · cause),
  and the buttons open the living zones of the one app.

## The graphics law (new decree, the core of this piece)
EVERY visual value derives from Acho and its mathematics:
- Colour ONLY by named band: tabla_semantica + comic_tokens + comic_accents (the house
  cores, atlas/puros/ + public/game/ of preceptoros-web). The sentence always says the
  state; colour never replaces the sentence.
- NO imported palette. The gentle reference measures ANATOMY (chat + buttons on top +
  composer), never colours, assets or marks.
- Motion: zero entrance animations; state changes instant or opacity <=80ms;
  prefers-reduced-motion respected.
- Accessibility of the invalid carbon (A11Y = contract A2A): 44px targets, focus-visible
  3px, aria-live threads, the 4 audio layers, no colour-only meaning.
- The local mini AI (1B/3B minis) must render and run it: vanilla JS, no build step, no
  heavy canvas, frames and receipts < 1,024 B.
